Research

Mapping the South Wales Coalfield with UAVs

Coal tips left behind by mining remain a long-term safety and environmental concern for communities across South Wales. I use repeat drone surveys, photogrammetry and change detection to identify ground movement and environmental change over time, and I link drone data with social science data to better understand community vulnerability. This work also sits at the centre of the outreach and community engagement I take part in across South Wales.

Research interests

  • UAV remote sensing and geospatial analysis
  • Photogrammetry and change detection for coal tips and other legacy mining landscapes
  • Linking drone data with social science data to understand community vulnerability
  • Web-based data infrastructure and data visualisation
  • Applying AI to geospatial data

Current work

  1. Reviewing the technical, environmental, regulatory and operational barriers to monitoring South Wales coal tips with UAVs
  2. Building a change detection workflow from repeat drone surveys of a coal tip site, testing how survey alignment, vegetation and a lack of ground control affect the detection of real ground movement
  3. Exploring a web-based mapping application for investigating the results
