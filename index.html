About

Three roles, one obsession: understanding systems deeply

Lecturer

For nearly 3 years I've taught Computing at the University of South Wales, covering C, C#, Python and PL/SQL, plus PowerShell scripting and a Windows Server module where I design and build networked systems - VMs, domain clients, and Linux clients integrated alongside them. I hold an FHEA and PgCertHE, and I contribute to outreach and recruitment, engaging local communities across South Wales.

YouTube Creator

I graduated with a First in ICT from the University of South Wales, a course focused on using computers rather than understanding them. I started Bare Metal Studio as a beginner trying to improve my own C skills, which pushed me toward systems programming and a deeper curiosity for how computers actually work. Now I combine that with my Higher Education teaching experience to explain C and systems programming clearly, from first principles. I'm currently working on a lexer, a 6502 emulator, and an HTTP server, all written in C, with sponsorships on the horizon as the channel grows.

Company Director (Coming Soon)

I lead OrbitGeo, a South Wales UAV surveying company delivering GIS analysis for a range of industries. It's currently dormant, but something I'm passionate about returning to - putting my UAV expertise to work for local communities.

Research

Mapping the South Wales Coalfield with UAVs

Alongside teaching, I research how UAVs can be used to map and monitor coal tips across the South Wales Coalfield - work that feeds directly into land management and public safety decisions for former mining communities. It's the same interest in aerial survey and GIS analysis that drives OrbitGeo, and it sits at the centre of the outreach and community engagement work I take part in across South Wales.
